# Sector 16 Game Guide

## Gameplay Overview
Sector 16 is a space exploration and survival game where you navigate a 64x64 grid of sectors, collect resources, trade, and upgrade your ship.

## Sectors
- **Trade Hub**: Safe zones where you can sell items, Nocturnium, and upgrade your ship.
- **Asteroid Belt**: Rich in Nocturnium Ore. Automated extraction upon discovery.
- **Ship Graveyard**: High chance of finding valuable space junk.
- **Nebula**: Hazardous sectors with radiation that can damage ship systems. Home to the Mysterious Wizard.
- **Ruin Sector**: Dangerous areas with high encounter rates and ancient guardians.
- **The Void**: Empty space with low discovery rates.

## Ship Systems
- **Power**: Determines your combat strength.
- **Defense**: Determines your shield strength.
- **Cargo**: Your ship's inventory capacity.
- **Locker**: Permanent storage at Trade Hubs.

## Special Locations
- **The Mysterious Wizard**: Found in Nebula sectors. He seeks a **Hydroponics Grow Light** (ID 39). Initially, he is visible on the map with a **purple wizard icon**. After your first meeting, he vanishes and will only appear by chance in Nebula sectors.
- **Upgrade Center**: Located in **The Nocturnal Hub** sector. It is marked with a **green trending-up icon** on the map. Here, you can permanently upgrade your ship using specific components found throughout the galaxy.

## Ship Upgrades
- **Cloaking Spell**: Obtained from the Wizard. Grants 100% success rate when using the **Avoid** action during encounters, and provides immunity to Nebula radiation.
- **Nocturnium Miner Upgrade**: Available at the **Upgrade Center**. Permanently increases Nocturnium mining yield in Asteroid Belts by +2.
    - **Required Components**: 
        - **Functioning Relay** (ID 17)
        - **Heavy Duty Cables** (ID 23)
        - **Atmospheric Scrubber** (ID 34)
    - **Installation**: All components must be in your ship's cargo hold to install the upgrade.

## Hazards
- **Radiation**: In Nebula sectors, there is a 20% chance per move to damage your **Shields**, **Weapons**, or **Cargo**.
    - **Shields Compromised**: Defense dice are limited to 1 during encounters.
    - **Weapons Compromised**: Attack dice are limited to 1 during encounters.
    - **Repair**: Systems can be repaired at any Trade Hub for a fee (20% of the previous upgrade cost).
    - **Alert**: A modal alert will notify you when your systems are compromised.
- **Pirates & Guardians**: Random encounters that can damage your ship or loot your cargo.

## Tips
- Collect Nocturnium in Asteroid Belts to fund your upgrades.
- Use the Storage Locker at Trade Hubs to keep valuable items safe.
- Find the Wizard to gain the Cloaking Spell for safe passage through dangerous sectors.
